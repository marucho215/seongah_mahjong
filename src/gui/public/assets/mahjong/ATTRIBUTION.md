## What was used

Only the `Regular/` variant's tile-face SVGs (38 files: Man1-9, Pin1-9, Sou1-9, one red-five
variant per suit (`*5-Dora.svg`), the seven honor tiles (Ton/Nan/Shaa/Pei/Haku/Hatsu/Chun),
and the tile back) were copied unmodified into `regular/` next to this file. The repository's
`Black/` variant and `Export/` (PNG) folder were not used.
